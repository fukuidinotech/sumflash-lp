SUMFLASH

Numbers flash by. Add them in your head.

Numbers appear one at a time, each for just a moment. Enter the total. This is flash mental math, the way it's practiced with the soroban (Japanese abacus).

See why you missed

After each answer, review every number that flashed along with the running total at that point. Numbers where a carry happened are highlighted, so you can spot exactly where you slipped. Replay the same problem, or replay it slowly.

How it works

  1. Tap StartNumbers appear one at a time.
  2. Enter the totalType your answer on the keypad.
  3. Result and reviewYour level goes up as you keep answering correctly and down when you miss.

Features

  • 30 levels that adjust automatically (1 digit × 3 numbers × 1 s up to 3 digits × 15 numbers × 0.5 s)
  • Set the speed yourself, anywhere from 0.2 to 5.0 seconds
  • Days practiced this week and your streak on the home screen
  • Numbers simply appear and disappear, with no animation getting in the way
  • Three themes (Minimal / Washi / Neon). Washi shows levels as grades
  • Separate profiles for each family member
  • Ranked play (1 digit × 10) with Game Center leaderboards
  • Numbers read aloud, and practice with subtraction mixed in
  • Records stay on your device. No sign-in

About ads

Ads appear at the bottom of the home screen and between sets, once every three sets. No ads while numbers are flashing, while you're entering your answer, or while you're reviewing. Premium has no ads.
